## How to read this

- **`pass1`** is the human's read of a source unit. A summary whose `pass1` is
  empty has not been read yet, and nothing may compress it.
- **`confidence`** is what survived a cold recall test — not familiarity.
  `solid` means it can be reproduced from memory; `shaky` means it was tested
  and did not hold; `unlearned` means pass 1 did not take and the source needs
  a re-read.
- **`status`** appears on synthesis pages only: `decided` or `open`.
